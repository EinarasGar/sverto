Sverto is a net worth tracker that keeps your spending and your investments in one ledger. It is an expense tracker and a portfolio tracker in one app, in every currency you use.

Most budgeting apps stop at your bank balance. Most portfolio trackers stop at your broker. Sverto records a food shop, a transfer to savings and an ETF purchase in the same place, so your net worth finally includes everything.

Salary in pounds. Spending in euros. Investments in dollars. One total.

Expense tracker and investment tracker, one ledger

• Record spending, income, transfers, investment trades, dividends and fees in one transaction history.
• Buy an ETF and Sverto tracks the cash that left and the units that arrived, together.
• Account balances, holdings and net worth are worked out from your transactions, so they always agree.
• Search your transactions and sort your spending into categories.

Net worth tracker that includes everything

• Bank accounts, savings, investments, pensions, crypto, property, mortgages and loans in one net worth total.
• A property counts at its value minus the loans linked to it. Credit cards and other debts are listed as liabilities.
• Follow your net worth over time, from a single day to your whole history.

Multi-currency from the start

• Amounts stay in the currency they happened in: pounds, euros, dollars or any other.
• Totals convert into the reference currency you choose.
• Open a currency to see its balance over time, the accounts holding it and its transactions.
• A multi-currency net worth tracker for people who live abroad, travel often or invest in overseas markets.

Portfolio tracker with cost basis

• Track stocks, ETFs, funds and crypto beside your everyday accounts.
• Open a holding to see its purchase lots, units, cost basis and current value.
• Realised and unrealised gains are shown separately, with sales matched to the oldest lots first (FIFO).
• Dividends and fees are part of the same history.

Statement import and receipts

• Link a supported investment account to bring in orders, dividends and cash movements.
• Import bank statements as CSV, PDF or screenshots, and review the changes before anything is saved.
• Photograph a receipt and get one transaction per item, each with its own category.
• Or add a transaction by hand in a few taps.

Myra drafts. You decide.

Ask Myra, the built-in assistant, about your own records: "How much did I spend on groceries last month?" or "Find my train tickets." Describe a purchase or a trade and Myra drafts the entry. Nothing is written to your ledger until you approve it, and you can switch Myra off in Settings.

Phones, tablets and foldables

On a tablet or an unfolded foldable, your list stays on one side and the item you open sits beside it. Move through transactions, accounts and holdings without losing your place.

Yours to keep

• Open source, so anyone can check how the numbers are worked out.
• Export your data whenever you like.
• No adverts.
• Prefer to run it yourself? Point the app at your own self-hosted server.

Who Sverto is for

People who invest as well as spend: someone paid in one currency and living in another, an investor with ETFs at one broker and shares at another, a household with a mortgage, savings and a pension. If you keep a spreadsheet to join up a budgeting app and a portfolio tracker, Sverto is built to replace it.

Sverto is a record-keeping tool. It does not give financial, investment or tax advice.
