Most budgeting apps stop at your bank balance. Most portfolio trackers stop at your broker. Sverto keeps spending, investments and net worth in one ledger, in every currency you use.

Salary in pounds. Spending in euros. Investments in dollars. A food shop, a transfer to savings and an ETF purchase all belong to the same financial picture, so Sverto records them in the same place.

One ledger for what you spend and what you invest

• Record purchases, income, transfers, investment trades, dividends and fees in one transaction history.
• Buy an ETF and Sverto tracks the cash that left and the units that arrived, together.
• Balances, holdings and net worth are worked out from your transactions, so they always agree.

Net worth that includes everything

• Bank accounts, savings, investments, pensions, crypto, property, mortgages and loans in one total.
• A property counts at its value minus the loans linked to it.
• Follow your net worth over time, from a single day to your whole history.

Every currency, one total

• Amounts stay in the currency they happened in.
• Totals convert into the reference currency you choose.
• Open a currency to see its balance over time, the accounts holding it and its transactions.

Know what you own and what it cost

• Track stocks, ETFs, funds and crypto beside your everyday accounts.
• Open a holding to see its purchase lots, units, cost basis and current value.
• Realised and unrealised gains are shown separately, with sales matched to the oldest lots first (FIFO).

Less typing

• Link a supported investment account to bring in orders, dividends and cash movements.
• Import statements as CSV, PDF or screenshots, and review the changes before anything is saved.
• Photograph a receipt and get one transaction per item, each with its own category.

Myra drafts. You decide.

Ask Myra, the built-in assistant, about your own records: "How much did I spend on groceries last month?" or "Find my train tickets." Describe a purchase or a trade and Myra drafts the entry. Nothing is written to your ledger until you approve it, and you can switch Myra off in Settings.

Room to work on iPad and iPhone Duo

On a larger screen your list stays on one side and the item you open sits beside it, so you can move through transactions, accounts and holdings without losing your place.

Yours to keep

• Open source, so anyone can check how the numbers are worked out.
• Export your data whenever you like.
• No adverts.
• Prefer to run it yourself? Point the app at your own self-hosted server.

Sverto is a record-keeping tool. It does not give financial, investment or tax advice.
