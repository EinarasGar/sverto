• Fold, unfold or rotate and Sverto keeps your place
• Quick upload takes photos, PDFs and other files, and splits a receipt into one transaction per item
• Import bank statements as CSV, PDF or screenshots
• Properties with linked loans, a Liabilities list and a page for each currency
