Sverto now uses the whole screen on iPad and iPhone Duo.

- On iPad, and on iPhone Duo unfolded and held sideways, your list stays on screen and the item you open appears beside it
- This works for transactions, accounts, holdings, properties and currencies
- Forms such as adding or editing a transaction take the full width, then return you to where you were
- Fold, unfold or rotate and Sverto keeps your place
- iPhone looks and works as before

Recently added, in case you missed it:

- Quick upload takes photos, PDFs and other files, and turns a receipt into one transaction per item
- Properties show their value minus linked loans, and other debts are listed under Liabilities
- Tap a currency to see its own page: balance over time, accounts and transactions
